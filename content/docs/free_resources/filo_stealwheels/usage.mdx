## Removing Wheels

1. Approach a vehicle.
2. (Optional) Lift the vehicle using **Filo Jack**.
3. Use a tire iron.
4. Select the wheel to remove.
5. The removed wheel is added to the player's inventory.

## Notes

- Wheels are handled individually.
- Filo Jack integration is optional.
- Tire iron durability can decrease after every use.
