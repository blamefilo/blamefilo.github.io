---
title: Usage
---

Approach either side of a vehicle.

Available interactions include:

| Action | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Attach Jack (Left) | Place a jack on the left side |
| Attach Jack (Right) | Place a jack on the right side |
| Pump Jack (Left) | Raise the left side |
| Pump Jack (Right) | Raise the right side |
| Detach Jack (Left) | Remove the left jack |
| Detach Jack (Right) | Remove the right jack |

## Notes

- Players must be close to the vehicle.
- Players must stand on the correct side.
- Both sides may be lifted independently.
- Removing one jack correctly preserves the remaining side.
